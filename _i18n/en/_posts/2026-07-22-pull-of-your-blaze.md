I wish it was you  
To put my heart into,  
To share the common space,  
To walk with at one pace.

I could've sung all day,  
Enjoyed the words you say,  
Admired features of your face  
Breathing in your scent of grace.

But do I have the right   
To get close to your light,  
To be trapped by your lure  
And render my soul pure?

I can't resist the draw  
Of your gravitation law.  
So there's only one thing  
That I ought to bring:

I will give you a place  
To settle down your blaze,  
So that you are free  
To celebrate your glee.
